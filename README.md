# CareerPilot AI

Adaptive AI Career & Interview Preparation Platform.

CareerPilot AI is designed to act as a persistent AI tutor, career guide, practice coach, and eventually an AI interviewer.

The core idea is not simply to provide answers. CareerPilot maintains a persistent candidate state and uses evidence from assessments, learning activity, quizzes, coding practice, and interviews to continuously adapt the candidate's preparation journey.
